Evaluation Criteria

How projects are evaluated

30% WEIGHT

Problem Formulation & Impact

Clarity of the real-world campus, industrial, or research challenge addressed and relevance to target users.

30% WEIGHT

Technical Execution & AI Depth

Soundness of software architecture, model integration, prompt engineering, dataset handling, and reproducibility.

25% WEIGHT

Demonstration & UX Clarity

Quality of the YouTube walkthrough: showing real working features, interface responsiveness, and clear audio narration.

15% WEIGHT

Team Documentation & Roster

Complete roll number verification for every contributing member and a concise, accurate technical abstract.

Checklist for Team Leads

Before you submit

CHECK 01 · ROSTER

Unique Roll Numbers

Ensure every team member’s full name and official DUET roll number (e.g. 26F-CS-044) are entered accurately without duplicates.

CHECK 02 · VIDEO VISIBILITY

Public or Unlisted YouTube Link

Set your YouTube video visibility to Public or Unlisted. Private videos cannot play inside the faculty or voter review panels.

CHECK 03 · REVISIONS

Post-Approval Edits

You can edit your roster or video link at any time. Note that modifying an already-approved project returns it to the faculty queue for re-verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Authentication & Voting FAQ

How do I sign in if I forgot my password or prefer not to use one? +

Open the sign-in modal on the main page and select either Email OTP (to receive a 6-digit one-time verification code in your inbox) or click Forgot password? to receive a password reset link.

Can a student vote more than once? +

No. The database enforces a strict one-vote-per-authenticated-voter policy. Once your vote is confirmed, your ballot is locked for the remainder of the competition.

Are individual voter choices visible to teams or administrators? +

Never. Administrators only see aggregate vote counts per project, and students see final standings only after faculty publish official results.

What happens if the submission deadline passes? +

You can still save your project details privately as a draft in your workspace, and submit it once the faculty committee opens the submission window.
