Structured Academic Workflow

From lab prototype
to university recognition.

Designed for clarity and fairness across student teams, faculty reviewers, and student voters.

PHASE 01 · REGISTRATION

Assemble & Document

Team leads register their group roster with verified university roll numbers, write a clear technical abstract, and attach a YouTube demo.

PHASE 02 · MODERATION

Faculty Quality Review

Instructors inspect submissions for completeness and video clarity, approving entries for exhibition or moving rejected demos to the Rejected Gallery.

PHASE 03 · EVALUATION

Verified Peer Voting

During the official voting window, authenticated students review project demos and cast a single vote. Standings remain sealed until publication.
